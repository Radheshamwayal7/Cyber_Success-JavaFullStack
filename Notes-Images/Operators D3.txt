The Bitwise operators operate bit by bit.

The bitwise operators convert the values to binary format, then they perform the task and then the result is converted back into the decimal format.

The following are the bitwise operators:
& bitwise AND
| bitwise OR
^ bitwise XOR
~ bitwise NOT: is calculated as -(n+1)
For example:
		byte m = -34;
		System.out.println(~m); // 33
		
<< left shift:
shifts the entire group of bits towards the left.

>> right shift:
shifts the entire group of bits towards the right.

Condition = Any programming statement or expression that returns true or false.
